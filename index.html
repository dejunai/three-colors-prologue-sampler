A BRIEF HISTORY
OF WIDOW'S BIGHT

As Presented by the Municipal Board
of Trade & Civic Improvement

— 1923 —

The Harbor of Widow's Bight — Gateway of Prosperity

· · ·

In the decade of the eighteen-twenties,
the town's whaling fleet put out
upon the northern grounds—

and did not return.

· · ·

Berths once crowded with the fleet — recorded vacant

· · ·

Of those few who came ashore,
no two accounts agreed.

Each telling differed from the last.
The matter was left unsettled.

· · ·

Municipal ledgers — testimony entered, filed, and closed

· · ·

Settlements from the insurers
arrived in due course—

and passed into the hands
of a small number of households,
thereafter foremost in civic life.

· · ·

Residences of standing — the town's enduring families

· · ·

That same season, a tidal surge
claimed a low island station
beyond the outer reef.

Charts were amended.
No further inquiry was required.

· · ·

WIDOW'S BIGHT

1923

A town of sound reputation,
and of unbroken continuity.
